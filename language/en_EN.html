Manual PostNL app for Shopify

1. Introduction

With the PostNL Shopify plug‑in you can easily ship your orders with PostNL. You can print shipping labels, register your shipment with PostNL and track the status of your parcels.

With this PostNL plug‑in, Dutch and Belgian merchants can create different types of shipments:

  • Parcels Netherlands: home delivery or pick‑up at a PostNL point
  • Parcels Belgium: home delivery or pick‑up at a PostNL point
  • Parcels within the EU
  • Parcels outside the EU
  • Letterbox parcels: to the Netherlands, Belgium, EU and non‑EU destinations

2. Installing the PostNL app

2.1. New installation

To install the PostNL plug‑in, follow these steps:

  1. In your Shopify environment, go to Apps
  2. Click the button Go to the Shopify App Store in the top right corner
  3. Select the desired shop if you manage multiple Shopify stores
  4. Type PostNL in the search bar and press enter
  5. Click the PostNL app and then click Install

2.2. Updating an existing installation

The app will automatically update as soon as a new version becomes available. No manual maintenance is needed.

2.3. Testing

We recommend going through the checkout process by placing a test order after installing and configuring the app to ensure the checkout experience meets your expectations.

After configuring the app, you can test registering shipments with PostNL and printing shipping labels. No fees will be incurred as long as the labels are not handed over to PostNL.

3 Configuration

Go to Apps > Installed Apps > PostNL to open the PostNL app. Click Settings in the top right corner to configure the app.

3.1. Account

3.1.1. API key

The first important part of the integration is the API key. In your PostNL Business environment you can find your customer data and API keys under Account → Company details → Manage API.

Note: There are separate API keys for Sandbox and Production. Make sure to enter the production API key here and not the sandbox key.

3.1.2. Customer information

In your PostNL Business environment you can find your customer information. Enter the customer number, customer code, location code, non-EU customer code type (the two letters), and the four-digit customer code for non-EU shipments in the Customer information section.

If you cannot find this customer information in your PostNL Business account, please contact your PostNL account representative.

3.1.3. Sender address

Enter the address of your company under Sender address.

3.1.4. Return address

When shipping from the Netherlands, a return label can be created using a reply number. When shipping from Belgium, the return shipment is created based on the sender address.

Depending on the country selected in the sender address, the return fields will be displayed. Enter your company’s return details here. Your return customer code is available in your PostNL Business environment or via your PostNL contact person.

Press Save during the process to store your configuration.

3.2. General settings

3.2.1. Default page selection

Here you specify whether you want to print the label using a standard printer (A4) or a label printer (A6). If you choose A4 here, you can specify the starting position on the A4 sheet when printing labels.

3.2.2. Description on label

With this option, you can add a description to the label in addition to the order number or order name. This description will be placed at the top, centered on the shipping label.

3.2.3. Fulfilment

You have the option to manually set the status of your orders to Fulfilled. This means that a manual action is required to update the order status. Prefer this to happen automatically? Then select the option Automatic. Once the parcel receives its first scan at PostNL, the status changes to Fulfilled.

3.2.4. Fulfilment notification

When the order is fulfilled, the recipient will receive an email with the shipment status.

3.3. Default export settings

With the default export settings you link the PostNL shipping method to the shipping zones you have created in Shopify. It is very important that the shipping zones match PostNL’s different shipping methods and that no shipping rates exist with the same name. This does not apply to 'PostNL afhaalpunten' for the Netherlands and Belgium, as the PostNL app creates these rates automatically. If duplicate names do exist, they must be completely deleted first and then recreated with a unique name.

For more information about international shipping products, visit the information page or contact your PostNL account manager.

3.3.1. Shipping method settings

When you click Manage for a shipping zone, a new window opens. Here you can choose whether you want to use the PostNL shipping methods. If you enable PostNL shipping methods, select which options you want to offer for each type of shipment.

  • Standard delivery Netherlands (from the Netherlands): Parcels are delivered to a home or business address in the Netherlands. The merchant is also located in the Netherlands.
  • Pick-up at a PostNL point Netherlands (from the Netherlands): Parcels are delivered to a PostNL pick-up point in the Netherlands. The merchant is also located in the Netherlands.
  • Standard delivery Belgium (from the Netherlands): Parcels are delivered to a home or business address in Belgium. The merchant is located in the Netherlands.
  • Pick-up at a PostNL point Belgium (from the Netherlands): Parcels are delivered to a PostNL pick-up point in Belgium. The merchant is located in the Netherlands.
  • Standard delivery Belgium (from Belgium): Parcels are delivered to a home or business address in Belgium. The merchant is also located in Belgium.
  • Pick‑up at a PostNL point Belgium (from Belgium): Parcels are delivered to a PostNL pick‑up point in Belgium. The merchant is also located in Belgium.
  • Delivery within the EU: For all shipments to EU destinations.
  • Delivery outside the EU: for all shipments to non-EU destinations.
  • (Letterbox) packets International / EU: For letterbox parcels and small parcels sent within or outside Europe.

Is your webshop located in the Netherlands? Always choose a shipping method from the categories marked with (from the Netherlands). This does not apply to European and worldwide shipping methods.

Is your webshop located in Belgium? Always choose a shipping method from the categories marked with (from Belgium). This does not apply to European and worldwide shipping methods.

3.3.2 Pick‑up points

Merchants with a Shopify Grow (formerly Shopify), Advanced or Plus plan can display PostNL pick‑up points on the Shipping page during checkout. For merchants with a Shopify Basic plan, the pick‑up points can be shown on the Thank‑you page.

Activate pick‑up points (Grow, Advanced and Plus)

When installing the PostNL app, a shipping rate is automatically created per shipping zone (go to Settings > Shipping & Delivery > General shipping rates). This rate, called PostNL pick‑up points (rates provided by the app), allows consumers to have their parcel delivered to a PostNL pick‑up location.

If you install the PostNL app first and only then create your Shipping Zones, the rate will not appear automatically, and you will need to manually add the PostNL pick‑up point rate.

  1. Click + Add rate under the shipping zone
  2. Select Use carrier or app to calculate rates
  3. Select PostNL pick‑up points (rates provided by the app)
  4. Select all Services. This ensures pick‑up points are displayed in checkout when a valid delivery address is entered.

Then, in the PostNL app, go to Settings > Default export settings and click Manage next to Netherlands – PostNL pick‑up point. Here you can determine whether PostNL pick‑up points are shown during checkout and set the price for this shipping method. In this menu, you can also specify which shipping method applies to pick‑up locations.

The following shipping methods are available for PostNL pick‑up points:

  • Pick‑up at a PostNL point Netherlands (from the Netherlands): for pick‑up points in the Netherlands; merchant address is in the Netherlands.
  • Pick‑up at a PostNL point Netherlands (from Belgium): for pick‑up points in the Netherlands; merchant address is in Belgium.
  • Pick‑up at a PostNL point Belgium (from the Netherlands): for pick‑up points in Belgium; merchant address is in the Netherlands.
  • Pick‑up at a PostNL point Belgium (from Belgium): for pick‑up points in Belgium; merchant address is in Belgium.

Save the settings and the pick‑up points will appear in your checkout.

Dynamic rates for Pick‑up Points (Grow, Advanced or Plus)

Want to set different rates for Pick‑up Points? You can! After selecting a shipping method, scroll down. First specify the minimum order amount required for customers to pick up their order at a PostNL point. Then choose whether you want to use (1) a fixed rate or (2) conditional rates. The shipping costs can vary based on the total order amount or the weight of the shipment. Add as many rates as you need. When dynamic rates are enabled, the fixed rate will be disabled.

Activating Pick‑up Points (Basic)

Using a Shopify Basic plan? Learn how to display PostNL pick‑up points on your checkout’s Thank You page.

If you installed the PostNL app before 1 February 2025, make sure to apply the update for the Thank You page and the Order Status page starting 2 September 2025. You can do this by following the steps below.

  1. Go to Settings in Shopify.
  2. Click Checkout.
  3. The required update will appear at the top.
  4. Perform the requested action.
  5. Then, under Checkout Settings, click Customize in the Configuration block.
  6. At the top center of the page, click Checkout.
  7. Select the Thank You page and publish it.
  8. Repeat step (7) for the Order Status page.

Now configure the Pick‑up Points:

  1. Create a shipping rate for PostNL pick‑up points via Settings > Shipping & Delivery > General shipping rates.
  2. Next, in the PostNL app, go to Settings > Default export settings and click Manage next to the shipping rate you just created.
  3. Choose the desired PostNL shipping product under Pick‑up at a PostNL point Netherlands (or Belgium).
  4. Save the selected shipping product.
  5. Save the PostNL settings.

If the customer forgets to select a pick‑up point on the Thank You page, they will receive a reminder email after 30 minutes. This allows them to select a pick‑up point afterwards.

Example: configuring shipping methods

My webshop is located in the Netherlands. For the Netherlands, I have created one shipping zone with two different prices: one price for standard delivery and one price for Pick‑up Points.

Next, I go to the [Settings of the PostNL app > Default export settings]. There I select, for PostNL Standard and PostNL Pick‑up Points, a shipping method that matches the Dutch shipping options.

For PostNL Standard, I choose one of the shipping methods from the category Standard delivery Netherlands (from the Netherlands).

For PostNL Pick‑up Points, I choose one of the shipping methods from the category PostNL Pick‑up Points. Note: here I also enter the price for Pick‑up Points (and not in Shopify’s Shipping & Delivery settings as is the case for PostNL Standard).

3.3.3 Shipping options

Some shipping methods include the following additional shipping options. Below you will find an explanation of what each option includes.

  • Signature on delivery: choose this option if you want confirmation that the order is delivered to the correct person.
  • Home address only: with this option, the order will not be delivered to neighbors.
  • Return if not home: with this option, the order will be returned immediately if it cannot be delivered to the recipient.
  • Increased liability: shipping valuable goods? With our increased liability option, the contents of your parcel are insured against damage or loss. Indicate the insured amount when using this option.
  • Notification (in combination with pick‑up at a PostNL point): with this option, the recipient will receive a notification when the parcel arrives at the PostNL point.

Do all your products fit through the letterbox (38 x 26.5 x 3.2 cm) and do they weigh no more than 2 kg? Then it is advisable to ship the orders as a letterbox parcel. This option is available for shipments within the Netherlands and both within and outside the EU.

It is also possible to send an individual order as a letterbox parcel.

See section 4.3.1 for more information.

3.4. Alternative shipping option

With this setting, you can apply a different shipping method for orders above a certain amount than the one configured under the default export settings.

Select Yes if you want to use the alternative shipping method. Then indicate from which order value the alternative shipping method should apply and select the desired option.

Note: If your customer places an order above the amount configured for the alternative shipping method, but chooses pick‑up at a PostNL point during checkout, then the alternative shipping method will NOT be applied.

3.6. Worldwide shipping

To create a customs form for worldwide shipments, the weight per parcel, country of origin and HS code are required.

These product attributes can be added in Shopify. Go to Products → open a product → scroll down to the Shipping section and enter the weight, country of origin, and HS code.

Note: It is important that each product has an HS code configured. If not, there is a risk that the parcel will not pass through customs.

4. Using the plug‑in

4.1. Creating a shipping label

Shipping labels can be created in the Shopify order overview or in the PostNL app order overview.

4.1.1. Shopify order overview

When you open an order in the Shopify orders overview, you’ll find the more actions button at the top of the page. From this menu, select the PostNL option Print label & pre‑announce. The shipment will then be pre‑announced and the shipping label will be downloaded.

You can also print labels and pre‑announce multiple orders at once. In the orders overview, select the orders you want to process. Then click the ··· (three dots) on the right and choose Print label & pre‑announce. All selected shipments will be pre‑announced, and multiple labels will download at the same time.”

4.1.2. PostNL order overview

Open the PostNL app and go to the orders overview. After opening an order, you will find the button Print label & pre‑announce at the top of the page. Click this button to download the label and pre‑announce the shipment to PostNL.

You can also print labels and pre‑announce multiple shipments at once. Select the desired orders in the PostNL app orders overview and click Print label & pre‑announce. To download a label without pre‑announcing the shipment, click Print label.

4.1.3. Track & Trace information for the consumer

The PostNL app automatically adds the correct Track & Trace link to the “Shipping updates” email that Shopify sends once an order is fulfilled.

It is important that, after generating the shipping label, you do not manually enter a Tracking number or Carrier when fulfilling the order. Leave these fields empty and click Fulfill item or click Mark as fulfilled when selecting orders in the Shopify orders overview.

4.2. Shipping labels for worldwide orders

When printing labels for orders outside the EU, note that three documents will be downloaded automatically. Make sure to print all three:

  1. The shipping label
  2. The CN23 form
  3. The CP71 form

Note: For shipments outside the EU, the weight, country of origin and HS code must be filled in for all products. See section 3.6.

4.3. Adjusting an individual shipment

If you want to send an order using a different shipping method than the one defined in the Default export settings, you can adjust this within the PostNL app. Open the desired order in the PostNL app. In the section Change shipping method or number of parcels you can modify the number of parcels or change the shipping method.

Change the number of parcels if the content of a single order is too large or too heavy to ship as one parcel. Select the number of parcels and click Change & print.

Do you want to apply a different shipping method for this specific order? Select the desired option. If you choose a method with increased liability, enter the insured value. Then click Change & print.

Note: If your customer chose to have the parcel delivered to a PostNL pick‑up point, do not change the shipping method to a home‑delivery method. Likewise, if the customer chose home delivery, do not change the method to pick‑up at a PostNL point.

4.3.1. Letterbox parcel

Does the shipment fit through the letterbox (38 x 26.5 x 3.2 cm) and does it weigh no more than 2 kg? Then it is recommended to send the order as a letterbox parcel. Change the shipping option to NL‑NL: Letterbox parcel and click Change & print.

4.3.2. Emailing a return label

Has your customer received the order but wants to return it? You can easily email the return label to the customer, or share a barcode so the customer can have the return label printed at a PostNL point.

We recommend creating a return shipment via the PostNL app Orders overview. Open the order and click Send return label in the top‑right corner. You then have two options:

  1. Email the return label to the customer: The label is emailed directly to the customer, who must print it themselves.
  2. Email a barcode to the customer (smart return): The customer receives a barcode that can be scanned at a PostNL service point to have the return label printed — ideal for customers without a printer.

6. Attachments – Error codes

Sometimes a specific functionality does not work because certain information is missing in the settings. Below you will find the most common error codes and their explanations.

6.1. General

Shipping method not recognized by the app. Export the shipment on the order detail page: No shipping method was selected for this order. Check whether the Default export settings have been saved correctly, or open the order in the PostNL app and adjust the shipping method under Change shipping method or number of parcels.

No labels will be printed; there is likely an issue with your settings: Usually appears together with the error above. This means a label cannot be generated because required information is missing, often under Default export settings.

No export settings found for the shipping method. Add them under Settings: A reminder that some required settings have not yet been configured.

API key is not valid: The entered API key is invalid or incorrect. The merchant should verify and re‑enter their API key.

For the Netherlands, the insurance limit is €5500.00 — please comply: The insured value entered for a shipment with increased liability exceeds the allowed limit.

For Belgium, the insurance limit is €500.00 — please comply: The insured value entered for a shipment with increased liability exceeds the allowed limit in Belgium.

6.2. Pick‑up (3_ _)

310 – No pick‑up locations found:
When the customer chooses to pick up the parcel at a PostNL point, they must select a PostNL pick‑up point on the Thank‑you page. This message indicates that no PostNL point was found. Ask the customer to check their postal code and search again for a PostNL point.

320 – No pick‑up location selected for this order:
The customer will receive a reminder email 30 minutes after placing the order to select a PostNL pick‑up point.

6.3. Return (4_ _)

410 – Cannot create return shipment for this order:
The country from which the return shipment originates is not valid for return shipments.

420 – Cannot create return shipment for this order:
The destination country of the return shipment is not valid for return shipments.

430 – A return label cannot be requested when no outbound shipment exists for this order:
If an order has no assigned shipping method, no return label can be created. A shipping method must be selected first.

440 – No email address found for this order:
Shopify did not provide an email address for the customer, so the return label cannot be sent.

450 – The reply number (NL) or street (BE) is required to create a return shipment:
The reply number or street name for the return address is missing in the settings.

6.4. Validation of product codes (5_ _)

510 – No export settings found for the shipping rate. Add them under Settings:
There is no Default export setting configured for this shipping rate. A default export setting must be selected before labels can be downloaded. Alternatively, open the order in the PostNL app and adjust the shipping method under Change shipping method or number of parcels.

520 – The shipping rate is not configured to ship with PostNL:
The merchant chose not to use a PostNL shipping method for this shipping rate.

6.5. Request (6_ _)

600 – General errors not specifically captured:

  • An issue occurred while saving.
  • An issue occurred while generating the label(s).
  • An issue occurred while printing the label(s).
  • An issue occurred while requesting a return label.
  • An issue occurred while creating a draft.

610 – PostNL returned a general error:
The error message returned by PostNL will be shown here.

620 – PostNL returned an error when generating the label:
The specific error message from PostNL will be shown here.

Example:

620: Countries in receiver and sender address not allowed together for this product.

When creating an additional parcel, a shipping option must be selected. If the merchant is located in Belgium and selects an NL‑NL shipping option, the request will fail. The same applies if a Dutch merchant selects a BE‑BE shipping method. Always ensure that the first part of the shipping method (BE‑BE, NL‑NL, BE‑NL, NL‑BE) represents the country where the webshop is located, and the second part represents the destination country.

6.6. Barcodes (7_ _)

710 – Cannot create barcode:
A barcode could not be generated for shipments within the Netherlands or Belgium. This may be caused by an incorrect customer number or customer code.

720 – Cannot create international barcode:
A barcode could not be generated for shipments within the EU or outside the EU.

6.7. Cover (8_ _)

810 – Enter a value to insure the alternative shipment:
An insured value could not be created because the amount was not provided.

6.8. International (9_ _)

910 – There must be a minimum of 1 order line and a maximum of 5 order lines in the order:
For an international shipment, the order must contain at least 1 and no more than 5 order lines. Each line represents one product, although each line may contain multiple units of that product.
